Account & Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your Triple A account and personal information at any time.

How Data Deletion Works

Under Section 24 of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and mobile application platform policies (Google Play & Apple App Store), you may request the deletion of your account and personal records.

Data Subject to Deletion Data Retained & Statutory Exception
  • Account profile, name, email, phone number
  • Login credentials & session tokens
  • Daily home tracking logs & symptom scores
  • Uploaded exercise form videos & photos
  • Subjective pet recovery comments
  • Statutory Veterinary Clinical Records: Formal clinical notes, SOAP assessment records, and treatment prescriptions created by your veterinary physiotherapist must be preserved by the attending clinic for the minimum statutory period (up to 5 years) in compliance with veterinary council guidelines.

Processing Timeline

Deletion requests are registered immediately, assigned a tracking reference number, and processed within 30 calendar days. A confirmation email will be sent to your registered address upon submission.
