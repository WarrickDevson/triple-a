FAQ

Common questions

How do I get access to Triple A?

Triple A is provided through participating veterinary physiotherapy clinics. Owners receive an invite code from their clinic to create an account in the owner app. Physiotherapists log in directly to the physio portal with credentials provided by their clinic.

Who is Triple A for?

Triple A serves two audiences: pet owners following home rehabilitation programmes, and veterinary physiotherapists who prescribe, monitor, and support recovery. It is designed for clinical rehabilitation — not general pet fitness.

Why are there two apps?

The owner app is optimised for daily exercise guidance, tracking, and communication on mobile. The physio portal is a clinical workspace for managing patients, building programmes, reviewing progress, and generating reports. Both connect to the same platform.

Is my data private and secure?

Triple A is designed in full compliance with POPIA (South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act). Personal information and clinical rehabilitation records are encrypted in transit and handled securely. Access is strictly limited to authorised clinic staff and your pet's assigned care team. Read our Privacy Policy or submit a Data Deletion Request anytime.

What species does Triple A support?

The platform supports multiple species including dogs, cats, and equine patients. Exercise templates can be filtered by species, and treatment plans are tailored to each patient's clinical needs.
