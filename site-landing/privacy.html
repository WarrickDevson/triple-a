1. Responsible Party & Commitment

Triple A (Animal Activity Assistant) ("we", "us", "our") operates the Triple A platform, including the Triple A Pet Owner Mobile Application, the Triple A Physio Clinical Portal, and associated web services at mytriplea.co.za.

We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding your privacy. We process personal information in strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act No. 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") of the Republic of South Africa and applicable international mobile application data protection standards.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Pet Owners utilizing the Triple A mobile app for guided home rehabilitation exercises, progress logging, video submission, and clinical messaging.
  • Veterinary Physiotherapists & Clinic Staff utilizing the Triple A Physio Portal for patient management, treatment plan design, SOAP clinical note recording, and progress evaluation.
  • Visitors accessing our public website and marketing landing page at https://mytriplea.co.za.

3. Categories of Personal Information Collected

We only collect personal information that is necessary for veterinary rehabilitation and clinical communication:

Data Category Examples Collected Primary Purpose
Account & Identity Full name, email address, password (cryptographically hashed), telephone number, clinic invite codes. Authentication, access control, linking owners to attending veterinary clinics.

4. Purpose of Processing & Legal Basis

Under POPIA Section 11, we process your personal information on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing required to provide the Triple A software platform, connect pet owners to their clinical physiotherapy practice, and enable digital exercise tracking.
  • Legitimate Clinical & Care Interests: Facilitating effective animal rehabilitation, injury recovery tracking, and collaborative clinical oversight.
  • Informed Consent: When you voluntarily register, upload videos, or opt-in to communications.
  • Statutory & Legal Compliance: Complying with veterinary record-keeping standards and South African financial/tax regulations.

5. Compliance with the 8 POPIA Conditions

Triple A is architected around the eight statutory conditions for lawful processing under POPIA:

  1. Accountability: We ensure all measures giving effect to the principles are implemented, maintained, and audited.
  2. Processing Limitation: Information is processed lawfully, in a reasonable manner that does not infringe privacy, and solely with adequate justification.
  3. Purpose Specification: Data is collected for explicitly defined, clinical and rehabilitation purposes.
  4. Further Processing Limitation: Personal information is not processed for any secondary purpose incompatible with initial collection. We never sell your personal information.
  5. Information Quality: Users and clinicians have direct self-service tools to update, correct, and ensure data completeness and accuracy.
  6. Openness: We maintain complete transparency regarding what data is collected, stored, and shared through this Policy.
  7. Security Safeguards: Personal data is protected against loss, damage, unauthorized destruction, and unlawful access through industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.
  8. Data Subject Participation: You maintain the absolute right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data under Section 24 of POPIA.

6. Voice Dictation & AI Processing Safeguards

The Triple A Physio Portal provides real-time voice dictation and AI-assisted SOAP note drafting to assist veterinary physiotherapists in documenting clinical assessments efficiently:

  • Local Speech Processing: Initial voice capture and speech transcription operate directly on the practitioner's device using local APIs.
  • No Permanent Ambient Audio Storage: Raw ambient audio files are not recorded or permanently archived on Triple A servers after transcription.
  • Clinical Privacy & No Training on User Data: Clinical notes sent for contextual veterinary term structuring are processed through secure, enterprise-grade AI APIs that do not use your private clinical records to train public foundation models.
  • Clinician Review & Approval: All generated notes require explicit review, editing, and sign-off by the licensed physiotherapist before saving into the patient record.

7. Technical & Operational Security Safeguards

We maintain comprehensive technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of data transmitted between mobile applications, web browsers, and backend APIs is encrypted using Modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: User passwords are encrypted using one-way adaptive cryptographic hashing algorithms.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict authorization boundaries ensure owners only access their own pets' records, and physiotherapists only access patients registered to their authorized clinic.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Platform services are hosted in enterprise data center facilities featuring physical access controls, continuous monitoring, and automated security patching.

8. Data Retention & Veterinary Record Obligations

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless prolonged retention is required or authorized by law:

  • User Account Information: Retained while your account remains active or until you request account deletion.
  • Rehabilitation Tracking & Media: Daily exercise logs and check-in videos are retained for the duration of the pet's rehabilitation cycle.
  • Statutory Veterinary Clinical Records: Formal clinical notes (SOAP records, clinical assessment reports, and official prescriptions) authored by veterinary physiotherapists form part of the animal's official health record. In accordance with South African veterinary statutory guidelines and professional regulations, attending clinics are legally mandated to retain formal clinical treatment records for statutory retention periods (typically up to 5 years).

9. Your Rights as a Data Subject under POPIA

Under Chapter 3 of POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request Access (Section 23): Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and receive a copy of that record.
  • Request Correction (Section 24): Request the correction or update of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, or incomplete.
  • Request Deletion / Destruction (Section 24): Request the deletion or destruction of personal information that we are no longer authorized to retain.
  • Object to Processing (Section 11(3)): Object on reasonable grounds to the processing of your personal information.
  • Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to submit a complaint to the South African Information Regulator if you believe your data protection rights have been infringed.

10. Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with POPIA Section 24 and Google Play / Apple App Store Data Safety policies, we provide a dedicated public portal where pet owners and practitioners can request full account erasure and personal data deletion without needing to reinstall or access the mobile app.
